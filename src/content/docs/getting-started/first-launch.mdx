## Choose your Launchpad

The first time you start Lunchpad, it shows **Choose your Launchpad**. Lunchpad asks every MIDI device to identify itself, so the list only shows Launchpads it can actually drive.

<AppShot name="device-picker" alt="First launch: a Launchpad X and a Launchpad Mini MK3 found, with the option to start without a Launchpad." />

Each entry tells you how the device was recognised:

- **Identified by device inquiry**: the Launchpad answered the identification request, often with its firmware version. This is the reliable way.
- **Identified by port name only**: the device did not answer, so Lunchpad went by the name of its MIDI port. The Launchpad S works this way.

Click **Connect** next to your Launchpad. The pads light up in a sweep and the main window opens with your first page.

If nothing shows up, plug the Launchpad in and wait a moment: the list updates on its own. **Scan again** looks once more. On Windows, close other apps that may hold the MIDI ports.

<Aside type="tip">
**Reconnect automatically** (on by default) remembers the Launchpad you connect and connects to it again when Lunchpad starts and whenever you plug it in. A remembered device is marked **remembered** in the list.
</Aside>

## No Launchpad? Use the pads on screen

Below the device list, **No Launchpad? Use the pads on screen.** lets you pick a **Layout** (any supported model) and click **Start without a Launchpad**. The pads in the window then run your macros when you click them, and you can switch to a real Launchpad any time.

This is also handy for building pages for a friend's Launchpad model.

## Make your first button

<Steps>

1. Right-click an empty pad and choose **Add button…**.

   A new button gets a random colour from the palette so you can tell it apart right away.

2. On the **Appearance** tab, type a **Caption**, for example `Hello`.

3. Switch to the **Actions** tab. Under **When pressed**, click **+ Add action** and open **Media → Text to speech**.

4. Type a text to speak, for example `Hello chat`. Click **Preview** to hear it.

5. Click **Save**.

6. Press the pad on your Launchpad (or click it on screen). Lunchpad speaks your text.

</Steps>
