Push-to-talk holds your voice chat's push-to-talk key while the actions between two markers run. Play a sound or a text-to-speech line into Discord or TeamSpeak, and your voice app transmits exactly as long as it plays.

## The two markers

Adding push-to-talk adds two rows at once: **Push-to-talk on** and **Push-to-talk off**. Drag the actions that should be heard between them. The markers keep their order; removing one removes both, and the actions in between stay.

The markers have no settings. The key is set once for all buttons.

## Set up the key

1. Open **Settings → Keyboard & mouse**.
2. Switch on **Hold a key while push-to-talk sections run**.
3. Under **Key**, press the same key your voice app uses for push-to-talk, and pick any keys to hold **With** it. A mouse button works too: the middle button or the side buttons.

## How it runs

- At **Push-to-talk on**, Lunchpad presses and holds the key, then waits 250 ms so the voice app notices before anything is heard. At **Push-to-talk off**, it releases the key and waits another 250 ms.
- Keep **Wait** on for the actions in between. If a Play sound has **Wait** off, the off marker follows right away and the key is released while the sound is still playing.
- Several macros share one key. It goes down with the first push-to-talk section that starts and comes up when the last one ends.
- If the setting is off or no key is set, the markers do nothing and add no pause.
- **Stop all macros** always releases the key.

<Aside type="caution">
If you stop a single macro while it is inside a push-to-talk section (with **Stop this macro** or **Restart this macro**), the key can stay held. Run **Stop all macros** to release it: as an action on a button, from the right-click menu of any pad, or with **Stop all running macros** in the tray menu.
</Aside>

<Aside type="tip">
Your voice app only hears what reaches its microphone input. To send a sound into voice chat, play it on a device the voice app records from, for example a virtual audio cable set as its input device, and pick that device in [Play sound](/actions/media/play-sound/).
</Aside>

On macOS, Lunchpad needs the Accessibility permission to press keys. See [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting/).
