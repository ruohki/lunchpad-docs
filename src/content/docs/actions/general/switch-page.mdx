Switch page shows another page on the Launchpad and in the window. Pages let one Launchpad hold far more buttons than it has pads: a page for scenes, one for sounds, one for your game.

## Settings

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Page** | Any page of your profile. If that page is removed later, the action shows "missing page" and does nothing. |

## How it runs

- The switch is immediate, and Lunchpad remembers the page: it is still showing after a restart.
- The macro keeps running after the switch. Actions after Switch page in the same list still run, and macros started on the old page carry on.
- In the actions that follow, "this button" still means the button that started the macro, on its own page.

## Examples

**A menu.** Give every page a button in the same spot, for example a round top-row button, that switches back to your main page.

**Peek at a page while holding a pad.**

1. **When pressed**: Switch page to **Sounds**.
2. **When released**: Switch page back to your main page.
