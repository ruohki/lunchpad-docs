Stop all sounds silences every sound Lunchpad is playing right now, no matter which button started it. Use it as a panic button for a clip that runs too long, or put it before a Play sound so sounds never pile up.

This action has no settings.

## How it runs

- It stops every sound from [Play sound](/actions/media/play-sound/) actions on every button. Those Play sound actions count as finished, so their macros go on with their next action.
- It stops sounds only. Text to speech keeps talking and the macros keep running. To end everything, use [Stop all macros](/actions/stop/stop-all-macros/), which also silences sounds and speech.

## Example

A soundboard where a new sound cuts off the previous one: on every sound button, put Stop all sounds first and Play sound second, as in the screenshot.
