The main window is a picture of your Launchpad. Every pad on screen is a pad on the device, and what you do in one shows up in the other.

<AppShot name="main-window" alt="The Stream page on a Launchpad X: scene buttons in the top grid row, a sound board row below them and a volume fader on column 8." />

## The header

The strip at the top holds the app name and the page tabs.

- Click a tab to switch to that page. The Launchpad repaints right away.
- The small number next to a page name is how many buttons it has.
- Double-click a tab to rename it.
- Right-click a tab for **Rename**, **Duplicate**, **Move left**, **Move right** and **Remove page**. The first page cannot be removed.
- **+** adds a page. With more than one page, the arrow next to it lists every page, which helps when the tabs do not fit.

More about pages in [Pages and backup](/guides/pages-and-backup/).

## Pads

- **Click a pad** to press it, just like pressing it on the Launchpad. Buttons run their actions; the physical pad lights up while you hold the mouse button.
- A pad with a running macro shows a pulsing outline.
- **Hover a pad** to read its [description](/guides/buttons/#description), if it has one — your own note about what the button does, with the current value of any variable it mentions.
- A small yellow dot in the corner means a sound file this button plays is missing.
- The round **gear** in the corner of the grid opens the settings.

## The right-click menu

Right-click a pad to edit it.

<AppShot name="context-menu" alt="The right-click menu on a pad." />

| Entry | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Edit button…** / **Add button…** | Opens the [button editor](/guides/buttons/). |
| **Copy**, **Cut**, **Paste** | Copy a button, including all its actions, and paste it on another pad or another page. |
| **New fader here…** | Starts a [fader](/guides/faders/) at this pad. |
| **Export button…** | Saves this one button as a file, to keep or to pass on. |
| **Import button…** | Puts the button from such a file on this pad, replacing what is there, after the same [review](/guides/pages-and-backup/#check-before-importing) a page import gets. |
| **Share on the hub…** | Publishes this button on the [community hub](/guides/pages-and-backup/#share-on-the-community-hub). Once shared from this pad, **Update on the hub…** appears beside it. |
| **Clear button** | Removes the button from the pad. |
| **Undo**, **Redo** | Step back and forward through your changes to the pages. |
| **Stop all macros** | Stops everything that is running. Shows how many macros are running. |
| **Buttons out of sight…** | Only when this page has buttons the connected Launchpad has no pad for. Opens the list below. |

On a fader, the menu offers **Edit fader…** and **Remove fader** instead. On a knob or a touch strip of a [Launchkey](/guides/launchkey/) it offers **Assign a fader…**, because those carry a value rather than a button.

## Buttons out of sight

Pages keep their buttons by position. Connect a Launchpad with a smaller grid and the buttons without a pad are kept, but there is nowhere to show them. **Out of sight** lists them, and you drag each one onto a free pad of the grid, exactly like moving a button inside the grid.

<AppShot name="outside-panel" alt="The Out of sight panel over the pad grid, listing two buttons that have no pad on this Launchpad, with a hint to drag them onto an empty pad." />

Open it from the notice (**Show them**) or from the right-click menu of any pad. The panel closes itself once the last button has a pad. See [using pages on another model](/getting-started/supported-launchpads/#using-pages-on-another-model).

## Move and copy with the mouse

<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd> on macOS) and drag a button to move it to another pad. Drop it on a pad that already has a button, and the two swap places.

<kbd>Shift</kbd> and drag to copy a button. Dropping a copy on a pad that already has a button replaces that button. <kbd>Alt</kbd> works for copying too.

Faders move the same way: <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> or <kbd>⌘</kbd> and drag any of their pads. A fader only lands where every one of its pads is free.

Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> during a drag to cancel it.

<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> or <kbd>⌘</kbd> and double-click a pad to open its editor directly.

## Undo and redo

Removing a page, clearing a button or removing a fader shows a short notice with **Undo**. The keyboard works too:

- Undo: <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Z</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>Z</kbd> on macOS)
- Redo: <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Y</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>Z</kbd> (<kbd>⇧</kbd> + <kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>Z</kbd> on macOS)

The shortcuts do nothing while an editor is open or while you type in a text field.

## Stopping macros

A macro that loops or waits for a long time keeps running until it ends or you stop it. To stop everything at once:

- right-click any pad and choose **Stop all macros**, or
- use **Stop all running macros** in the tray menu, or
- give a button the [Stop all macros](/actions/stop/stop-all-macros/) action.

## Developer mode

**Settings → Interface → Show diagnostics in the main window** adds a side panel next to the pads. It is off by default so the window is just your pages.

<AppShot name="developer-mode" alt="The main window with the diagnostics side panel: connection status, device facts, MIDI monitor and shared variables." />

The panel shows:

- the connected model, its firmware and MIDI ports,
- how many pads the device has and whether it has RGB or red and green LEDs,
- your **Shared variables** and their current values,
- **Last press**: the last pad you pressed, with its MIDI note, velocity and pressure,
- the **MIDI monitor**, a live list of the messages the Launchpad sends.

The header also shows the connection status while developer mode is on.

<Aside type="tip">
The MIDI monitor is the quickest way to check that a pad reaches Lunchpad at all. If pressing a pad adds nothing to the monitor, the problem is between the Launchpad and your computer.
</Aside>
