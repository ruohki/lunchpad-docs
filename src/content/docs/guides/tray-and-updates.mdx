Lunchpad runs its macros in the background. The window is only for editing: close it, and your pads keep working as long as Lunchpad sits in the tray.

## The tray menu

<Tabs syncKey="os">
  <TabItem label="Windows">
    The Lunchpad icon sits in the notification area of the taskbar. Click it to open the menu.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="macOS">
    The Lunchpad icon sits in the menu bar. Click it to open the menu. Clicking Lunchpad in the Dock also brings back a hidden window.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

| Entry | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Join the Discord!** | Opens the Lunchpad Discord in your browser. |
| **Show Lunchpad** | Brings the window back. |
| **Stay on top** | Keeps the window above other windows. |
| **Minimize to tray** | Closing the window hides it instead of quitting. |
| **Run at startup** | Starts Lunchpad when you log in. |
| **Stop all running macros** | Stops every macro that is running. |
| **Quit** | Ends Lunchpad. Macros stop and the Launchpad goes dark. |

The checkmarks match the switches in **Settings → Interface**, where **Minimize to tray** is called **Close to the tray** and **Run at startup** is called **Start Lunchpad with the system**. Changing one changes the other.

## Start with your computer

For a Launchpad that is always ready:

1. Turn on **Start Lunchpad with the system** (or **Run at startup** in the tray).
2. Turn on **Close to the tray**, so closing the window does not quit.
3. Optionally turn on **Start hidden in the tray** in **Settings → Interface**, so Lunchpad starts without opening its window.

With **Reconnect automatically** on (the default), Lunchpad connects to your Launchpad as soon as it starts, or when you plug it in later.

<Aside type="caution">
With **Close to the tray** off, closing the window quits Lunchpad and your pads stop working. Use **Quit** in the tray menu when you really want to end it.
</Aside>

## Updates

Installed copies of Lunchpad look for a new release on GitHub a few seconds after they start. When there is one, a notice **Update available** appears with **Install and restart** and **Later**.

- **Install and restart** downloads the new version, installs it and restarts Lunchpad.
- **Later** hides the notice. You can install from **Settings → About Lunchpad** any time.

**Check for updates** in **Settings → About Lunchpad** looks for a new version right now and tells you if you already have the latest.

All releases, with their notes, are on the [releases page](https://github.com/ruohki/lunchpad/releases).
