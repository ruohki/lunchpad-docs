## The Launchpad does not show up

- Plug it in and wait a moment. The device list updates on its own; **Scan again** looks once more.
- Try another USB cable or port. Some cables only charge.
- Make sure your model is [supported](/getting-started/supported-launchpads/).
- **On Windows**, only one program can use a MIDI port at a time. Close Ableton Live, Novation Components, another controller app or a browser tab with Web MIDI, then scan again.

If the Launchpad shows up as **Identified by port name only**, it did not answer the identification request. That is normal for the Launchpad S. For other models it can mean the port is busy or the device is still starting up; unplug and plug it in again.

If it never appears at all but the computer does see its MIDI ports, use **Not listed? Connect by hand.** at the bottom of the device list: pick the model and the two ports yourself. See [Connect by hand](/getting-started/first-launch/#not-listed-connect-by-hand).

## A Launchkey's keys or touch strips do nothing

The pads and buttons of a [Launchkey](/guides/launchkey/) arrive on its DAW port, but the **keys and the two touch strips use its ordinary MIDI port**, which Lunchpad opens alongside. If that port is busy, everything else works while the keys and strips stay silent.

- Close whatever else holds the port: your DAW, Novation Components, another controller app, a browser tab with Web MIDI. On Windows only one program can hold a MIDI port at a time.
- Then disconnect and connect again from the device list.

If only *some* keys do nothing, the keyboard is at another octave. Lunchpad knows the keys at the default octave; press **Octave −** or **Octave +** on the device until you are back, since Lunchpad cannot see the shift.

## The Launchkey keeps switching back

While a Launchkey Mini MK4 is connected, the surface belongs to Lunchpad: change the pad layout or the encoder mode at the device, or switch **Arp** or **Scale** on there, and Lunchpad puts it straight back. Disconnect in **Settings → Launchpad** to use the keyboard on its own again.

## Pads light up on screen but nothing happens on the device, or the other way round

- Open **Settings → Launchpad** and click **Repaint LEDs**.
- Turn on developer mode (**Settings → Interface → Show diagnostics in the main window**) and press a pad. If the **MIDI monitor** stays empty, the Launchpad does not reach Lunchpad: check the cable and close other MIDI apps.
- **Disconnect** and connect again from the device list.

## Keys are not pressed (macOS)

Hotkeys and push-to-talk need the Accessibility permission.

1. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility**.
2. Switch Lunchpad on. If it is already on, switch it off and on again after an update.
3. In Lunchpad, click **Check again** on the notice, or **Settings → Keyboard & mouse → Check keyboard access**.

## Soft presses are missed, or pads trigger too easily

On velocity-sensitive Launchpads, presses below a threshold are ignored. Open **Settings → Launchpad**, turn on **Custom press threshold** and move the slider: lower if soft presses are missed, higher if pads fire when you brush them. The default is 25.

On a **Launchpad Pro**, the press force comes from the Pro's own set-up screen. Hold **Setup** and check the velocity option in the third grid row from the top. With **Off**, every press counts as full force, so **Volume follows velocity** has no effect.

## A pad shows a small dot

The dot means a sound file this button plays is missing: it was moved, renamed or deleted, or the page came from another computer. Open the button, expand the **Play sound** action and choose the file again. A notice also lists the missing files.

## Buttons disappeared after switching Launchpads

Buttons are kept by position. If the new Launchpad has no pad at that position, the button is hidden, not deleted. A notice tells you how many, with **Show them**: that opens **Out of sight**, where you can drag each button onto a free pad of the smaller grid. The same list is in the right-click menu of any pad, and **Settings → Pages & backup** shows the count per page. Connecting the bigger Launchpad again also brings them back. See [Using pages on another model](/getting-started/supported-launchpads/#using-pages-on-another-model).

## OBS Studio does not connect

- Make sure OBS is running, version 28 or newer.
- In OBS, open **Tools → WebSocket Server Settings**, switch on the server, and compare the port and password with **Settings → OBS Studio** in Lunchpad.
- The **Status** line in the OBS tab shows the error OBS reports.
- If OBS runs on another computer, enter that computer's address as **Host** and check that a firewall lets the port through.

## Streamlabs Desktop does not connect

- Make sure Streamlabs Desktop is running.
- On the same computer, leave **Host** and **Port** at their defaults. Nothing needs to be switched on in Streamlabs.
- For Streamlabs on another computer, turn on **Remote Control** in Streamlabs (**Settings → Remote Control**) and copy its token into **API token**.

## Home Assistant does not connect

- The status line under **Settings → Home Assistant** shows the reason. **the access token was rejected** means the token is wrong or was deleted: create a new one on your Home Assistant profile page, under **Security**.
- **could not connect** or **timed out**: check the **Home Assistant address**, including `http://` or `https://` and the port, by opening it in a browser on the same computer.
- For an `https://` address with a self-signed certificate, switch on **Accept a self-signed certificate**.
- A new entity is missing from the suggestions: click **Reload entities**.

More in [Home Assistant](/guides/home-assistant/#when-it-does-not-connect).

## OBS, Streamlabs or Home Assistant actions are missing from the menu

The **+ Add action** menu only shows integrations you have switched on. Turn on **Connect to OBS Studio**, **Connect to Streamlabs Desktop** or **Connect to Home Assistant** in the settings.

## A macro keeps running

Loops and long waits run until they end or you stop them. Right-click any pad and choose **Stop all macros**, or use **Stop all running macros** in the tray menu. To make a button stop itself, see [Stop this macro](/actions/stop/stop-this-macro/) and [Repeat while held](/guides/action-lists/#repeat-while-held).

## Lunchpad is gone when I close the window

With **Close to the tray** off, closing the window quits Lunchpad. Turn it on in **Settings → Interface**. See [Tray and updates](/guides/tray-and-updates/).

## Something changed and I want it back

- <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Z</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>Z</kbd> on macOS) undoes changes to your pages while no editor is open.
- **Settings → Pages & backup → Restore previous profile** brings back the profile as it was before the last change.

## Logs and the diagnostics report

Open **Settings → Diagnostics**:

- **Copy report** copies a summary of versions, your system, MIDI ports, audio devices and integrations.
- **Open log folder** shows the log files.
