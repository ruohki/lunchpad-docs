## Every pad is a button

A button has a look, a colour and lists of actions: one for the press, one for the release, one for a long hold. Pages hold as many layouts as you like; the active page is painted onto the Launchpad, and the macros keep running while the window sits in the tray.

## Made for the Launchpads you have

Launchpad Mini (MK1, MK2, MK3), Launchpad S, MK2, X, Pro MK2 and Pro MK3 — and the Launchkey Mini MK3 and MK4, whose keys, knobs and touch strips drive macros too. Lunchpad asks every MIDI device who it is, so it finds your device no matter what the system calls its ports, and anything it cannot name you can still connect by hand.
